Binary Orbits Reveal Mass

Two stars orbit a shared centre of mass. Their motion reveals the mass of each star.

The Mass Ratio

M₁ — smaller orbit a₁M₂ — larger orbit a₂
M1M2 = a2a1
The more massive star moves in the smaller orbit. The barycentre sits closer to it.

Kepler’s Third Law

M1 + M2 = 4π²a³GP²
a = a1 + a2
Measure a and P to find the total mass. In solar units: M = a³ / P² (a in AU, P in years).
