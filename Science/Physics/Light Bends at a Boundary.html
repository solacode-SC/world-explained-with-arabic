Light Bends at a Boundary

Light changes speed between materials. Snell’s law tells us by how much it bends.

Speed in a Medium

v = cn
Watch the dashes: they are shorter and slower in the denser material. Light bends toward the normal when it slows down.

Snell’s Law

n1 sinθ1 = n2 sinθ2
