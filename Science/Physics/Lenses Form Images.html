Lenses Form Images

A thin lens bends parallel rays through its focus. Where the rays meet is the image.

What Kind of Image?

Beyond 2f the image is real, inverted and smaller. Between f and 2f it is real, inverted and larger. Inside f it is virtual, upright and larger (a magnifying glass).

The Lens Equation

1f = 1do + 1di
Magnification m = −dᵢ / dₒ. A negative m means the image is upside down.
